# Senior-editor QA review

This sheet covers the judgement calls that gates can't make. It merges the B.4 QA section of every module, with duplicates removed. The questions are in the order a reviewer watches: first frame, then the hook (0–3 s), then the body, then the CTA and ending, then whole-ad passes. Watch on a phone at full speed first, then scrub. Mark each line **OK**, or write a **note** with its timecode. Any note becomes a fix and, if it can recur, a rule (see RULEBOOK §11).

Precedence when judging: the user's inspiration edits (and the style profile measured from them) come first, then ES, then the video sources. Where a line below names a look (accent colour, grade, transition kind), read it as "the client's" look; the examples are from preset `profitcast-dark`.

---

## A. First frame (paused on frame 0, then the first 5 frames)
| Area | Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stop power | Would my thumb stop here? | Motion is already running (push, punch or animated card), and the look's brightest element (e.g. a glow, accent or white type) stands out against the A-roll. It doesn't look like every other selfie in the feed. | 4.2, 4 B.7#4 |
| Content | What is on frame 0? | The face, clearly visible, and/or the hook text. No black, no logo sting, no greeting card. | 9.8, 9.1, 4.2 |
| Cover frame | If this were the paused cover, would it read? | A large recognisable face, a lively expression, one clear element of intrigue, readable at thumbnail size. | 6.9 |
| Designed | Does the paused frame look finished? | Aligned, balanced, one focal point, no stray layers. | 4.3 |

## B. Hook (0–3 s)
| Area | Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clarity | After 2 s, do I know what this is about and who it's for, with sound on and with sound off? | The target viewer could name the topic in a few words (e.g. "this is about getting found online"). | 4.1, 9.8 |
| First word | Does the first word push into a sentence? | "Your…", "If…", "This…", a number. Not "Hi", "Hello", "Today". | 9.8 |
| Question | What question is in my head at 3 s, and do I care? | One clear, relevant question ("why do they get the calls and not me?"), not "who is this?". | 4.2 |
| Intrigue | Is the intrigue earned or cheap? | A real tension (lost calls, a hidden ranking problem). No "you won't believe". | 9.8, 4.1 |
| One reading | Could the hook be misread? | Only one interpretation. | 4.1 |
| Promise and proof | Is there proof, or only a promise? Is the stake added after the topic? | The topic is confirmed, then a stake or teaser is added. A real number or result is hinted at early if the script has one. | 4.6, 6.8, 6.1 |
| Likability | Do I like and trust this person? | Smiling, warm, well lit, clearly helping, talking to me at eye level. | 4.2, 7.3 |
| Visual change | Does something change every second or so without feeling frantic? | Cuts, punch steps, caption pages and build steps carry the eye; the face stays the anchor. | 6.9, 4.8 |

## C. Body (problem → agitation → proof → offer)
### Story
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Read only the beat-boundary sentences aloud. Is it "so… but… so…"? | Each boundary answers or complicates the one before; no "and also". | 6.3 |
| Is the problem a want I have, blocked by something specific? | Want = calls; obstacle = their listing beats mine. | 6.1 |
| Is there a moment where something new happens? | The reframe ("not because they're better") or the proof reversal changes direction. | 6.6 |
| Is the proof real and specific? | Named client, concrete number, ideally evidence on screen (review card, call log). | 6.8, 6.7 |
| Does every promise in the offer answer something I was told was wrong? | Each feature visibly pays off a problem item. | 6.7 |
| Do I always know what I'd miss if I swiped now? | A next payoff is set up at every beat. | 4.6 |
| Does the energy sag after the hook? | Each boundary brings something new; nothing coasts. | 6.9, 4.4 |
| Does the curve go down, then up? | Mild tension in the problem, a clear lift at the proof; no bottomless fear. | 6.5 |
| Is any exposition sitting there as "information"? | Facts arrive inside emotion or as a reveal, not as a brochure read. | 6.6 |
| Does it feel honest? | No fake urgency, no hype the content doesn't back. | 4.7, 6.8 |
| Does it feel native to the feed, not over-produced? | A real person talking to camera, one natural moment kept, technically clean. | 6.9 |

### Cuts and pace
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| What do I feel at each cut, and is it the feeling this beat needs? | Curiosity in the hook, a pang at the problem, confidence at the proof, ease at the CTA. No gimmick during a sincere line. | 2.1 |
| Does every cut move the story? | Remove any cut and something is lost. | 2.1, 2.2 |
| Is each cut on time? | Returns land as a new thought starts. No dead frame, no clipped syllable. | 2.1 |
| Did I notice any cut on the A-roll? | Cuts feel like the speaker leaning in or settling back; no twitch, no teleport. | 7.5, 2.4 |
| Does the pace have a shape? | A lift into the list or proof, a calm beat before it, a settle into the CTA. Not a metronome, not breathless. | 2.6, 2.5, 4.5 |
| With a click on every cut, does the rhythm sound designed? | Sparse, then dense, then sparse; not an even tick, not random clusters. | 2.5 |
| Where does my attention dip? | No stretch where both picture and idea stand still (check the longest stretch QC reports). | 4.4, 4.6 |
| Is each transition saying the right thing? | Whip = momentum, punch = emphasis, nothing like a template preset. | 2.3 |
| Does the edit stay in the speaker's perspective? | Cutaways are "what she's talking about"; returns to the face are her owning it. | 2.8 |
| Would a stranger follow it with no context? | Business type, place and offer are said and shown. | 2.1, 9.3 |
| Does it feel human-made rather than AI-made? | Cut choices respond to meaning, not to a fixed interval or template. | 2.8, 1.1 |

### Graphics and motion
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| In each frame, what do I see first, second, third? | Exactly the intended order, with one focal point. | 3.1, 3.2 |
| Squint: can I see the groups before reading? | Each group is one blob, headings belong to their bodies, captions are separate from cards. | 3.6 |
| Does every item land on its word? | The settle lands on or just after the word; voice and motion feel like one event. | 1.1, ES §1 |
| Where is my eye at every moment? | On one thing: the newest spoken item or the face. | 1.6, 1.2 |
| Does each element have weight? | Decelerates into place, pops overshoot slightly and settle; nothing stops dead or drifts forever. | 1.1, 1.3 |
| Did I see the start of every move? | A graphic that moves where I wasn't looking pulled my eye there first. | 1.5 |
| Do hero beats breathe and hit? | A calm moment before the price, the number or the logo; the hero lands harder than list items. | 1.1, 1.3 |
| Does anything appear from nowhere? | Every pill, tick and card grows, slides or is revealed. | 1.6 |
| Does anything shake? | Punch-ins, scaled text and drifting stages are perfectly smooth. | 1.3 |
| Is there life in holds? | A slow push or breathing element; never a frozen still. | 1.2 |
| Does it all feel like one designer made it? | Same type, sizes, colours, brackets, card behaviour and motion vocabulary in every cutaway. | 3.1, 1.6 |
| Is the accent meaningful? | Only the item being said is in the accent colour; the eye jumps there. | 3.1, 7.6 |
| Is anything crammed, floating or hugging an edge? | Neither murky nor disconnected; nothing near the frame edge or Reels UI. | 3.5 |
| Is the off-centre composition intentional? | It has an anchor; covering a part breaks the balance. | 3.6 |
| Is every graphic earning its place? | You could name the word each one illustrates; nothing is wallpaper. | 4.3, 7.8 |
| Does it read at phone size while scrolling? | One idea per frame, readable at a glance. | 3.1, 1.2 |
| Would anyone call any motion "template-y"? | No random bounces, no identical simultaneous moves, no effects for their own sake. | 1.1, 1.6 |
| Do transitions look like the motion that caused them? | Each transition kind the style uses reads as its motion: zoom-whips blur radially, glitches are short, fades are brief. | 1.7, ES §1 |

### Framing
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Does the speaker feel like they're talking to me? | Eye level, eyes on the upper third, centred, steady; no sideways slide between shots. | 7.3, 7.7 |
| Is the framing doing emotional work? | Tighter on claims and the CTA; the one big punch on the turning line. | 7.2 |
| Is there air above the head that says nothing? | Tight headroom, room for captions where the style puts them. | 7.1 |
| Is anything competing with the face? | Cards, plates and captions are dimmer or smaller than the face. | 7.1, 3 B.4 |
| Is a gaze or gesture answered? | A glance or point to screen-right is followed by a graphic from the right. | 7.5 |
| Does the depth moment read as 3-D? | Clear person, card, wall; clean edges, no aura. | 7.1, ES §2 |
| Is the restraint right? | Each effect has a reason. | 7.6 |

### Colour
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Does skin look like skin? | Warm and alive; no green beard edge, magenta forehead or red patches. | 8.5 |
| Does the face pop without looking cut out? | Separated by luminance (and warmth, if the grade is warm), with no halo. | 8.5, ES §2 |
| Across each A-roll cut, does anything change except framing? | Brightness, white balance and saturation are identical; the light never seems to change. | 8.4, ES §2 |
| Coming out of a cutaway, is it the same world? | The A-roll returns at the same level; the cutaway palette is a deliberate counterpoint. | 8.4, 8.2 |
| Is the image flat or crushed? | Real blacks with texture in hair and beard; no milky areas. | 8.7 |
| Does the wardrobe fight the grade? | Shirt and skin don't merge into one muddy family. | 8.8 |
| Is the vignette felt, not seen? | Darker corners, no grey ring, no spotlight. | 8.7 |
| Do colour meanings stay consistent, and does the accent grow toward the answer? | Accent = solution/brand, ✕ = problem, gold (or the brand colour) = client; the accent strongest at the end card. | 8.1, 8.3 |
| Does colour make me feel what the script wants? | Trust lines calm, problem lines urgent, the CTA pops. | 3.7 |

### Sound
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| On a phone speaker at mid volume, can I follow every word without captions? | No push-to-understand moments, and the music still feels full. | 5.6 |
| Does the music feel big enough for an ad, without sitting on a word? | It swells in gaps and cutaways and tucks under syllables; no audible pumping. | 5.6, 5.5, USER |
| Does the bed sound "a little quiet"? | If yes, raise it toward −5/−4 LU and deepen the voice-band carve (the words gate must still pass). | 5.5, USER |
| Does the voice sound like a close podcast mic? | Even tone, no boxy ring, no harsh "s", no room tail. | 5.8, ES §3 |
| Does the voice level jump between sentences? | Even, as if one recording. | 5.5 |
| Does the music push the right speed and character? | Never slower than the speaker; it suits the brand. | 2.6, 2.8 |
| Does music and sound tell me how to feel at each beat? | Energy shifts at the problem → solution turn; the proof gets the biggest hit. | 4.5, 4.6 |
| Is there a moment the energy drops so the next beat lands? | One clear dip before the payoff (if designed); the mix isn't uniformly loud. | 5.7 |
| Does every SFX belong to a visible event, and to one family? | Whoosh = whip, pop = item; same texture throughout; nothing cheap or off-genre. | 2.7, 5.4 |
| Is anything over-accented or audibly repeated? | Big hits only on the hook and offer/CTA; ticks vary naturally. | 5.1 |
| Are there sonic dead spots? | Every card has sound; no silent graphics. | 5.1 |
| Do transitions feel motivated by sound? | Sound moves slightly ahead into the cut. | 5.2 |
| Does it hold up on phone, headphones and a bigger speaker? | Clear on all; not muddy, not harsh. | 5.5, 5.2 |

### Captions
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Can I read every caption without pausing? | Each chunk stays until it has been read comfortably; nothing sits under the CTA button. | 9.8 |
| Does any caption cross the face? | A constant position (the style's `captions.position`, by default under the chin), never over the mouth or eyes. | 9.5 |
| Is there anything slack? | Every line starts on a meaningful word; no "so, um". | 9.5, 9.7 |

## D. CTA and ending
| Question | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Is the payoff satisfying, and does it come before the ask? | The answer lands, then the CTA; no dangling question. | 4.2 |
| Does the tension release after the peak? | Cutting relaxes; the offer and CTA feel settled and trustworthy, not breathless. | 2.5 |
| Is the CTA complete and unhurried? | One clear ask; the full sign-off (the whole brand name). | 2.6, USER |
| Does the ending leave a high? | The last moment is the warmest or most confident line, with the brand on it. | 6.9, 6.5 |
| When does it feel over, and is that the real end? | The ending feeling arrives with the sign-off; no tail, no second CTA. | 9.8, 6.8 |
| Does the music end designed? | It resolves on a tail or hit, not a slammed stop, and nothing plays on black. | 5.4 |
| On replay, does the loop jar? | Last frame to first frame feels like a cut, not a crash to black. | 9.1 |
| Is the CTA frame the calmest, most spacious frame? | Yes, with the accent on the action. | 3.6, 3.1 |

## E. Whole-ad passes
| Pass | What "good" looks like | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Sound off | Story, offer and CTA are fully understood from picture and captions. | 2.7, 9.5 |
| Reels UI overlay | Nothing important is under the top bar, the bottom panel or the right rail. | 9.4 |
| Fresh eyes | Someone who hasn't seen the cut can say the problem, the proof and the ask, and doesn't drift in the agitation list. | 6.2, 2.1 |
| Native look | It reads as a vertical Reel, not a cropped YouTube video; no bars, no dead space. | 9.1, 9.6 |
| Inspiration A/B | Side by side with the nearest inspiration edit, it looks like the same series. | USER |
| Cause before fix | For any note at second X, check whether the viewer was missing information earlier. | 2.1 |
| Churn check | Revisions target failed gates or notes, not taste churn. | 6.6 |
