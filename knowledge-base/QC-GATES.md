# QC gates (master table)

This is the single list of gate rules (183; RET-24 to RET-32 were added with viral mode, docs/VIRAL.md; SND-32 and SND-33 with senior sound, docs/SOUND.md). It merges ES §4 and the B.5 section of every module, with duplicates removed; the old id of every source gate maps to one row here (index at the bottom). Scripts read this file. `cves qc` measures 96 of them automatically today; the rest are checked by eye (contact sheets, the moodboard) or are not measurable yet.

**Thresholds that are per-client choices come from the style profile, not from this file** (`docs/STYLE.md`): cutaway share (`grammar.cutaway_share`), the longest span without the face (`grammar.max_cutaway_s`), roto count (`grammar.roto_max`), bed level (`sound.bed_lu`), master loudness (`sound.master_lufs`, `sound.true_peak`), punch-in step (`framing.min_step`), transition kinds (`transitions.*`), caption look and position (`captions.*`), safe zone and margin (`platform.safe_zone`, `platform.margin`), duration (`qc.duration` / `platform.duration`) and the palette roles (`colors.*`). Where a row below quotes a number for one of these, it is the default or the value from preset `profitcast-dark`, where CVES was first tested.

**Status**
- **BLOCKING**: well supported and objective. A failure stops delivery.
- **WARNING**: reported; a human decides.
- **CALIBRATE**: the threshold must first be measured on the user's inspiration edits (and later on Meta data). It reports until then, then becomes BLOCKING or WARNING.

**Implementation**: **EXISTS** names the script in `Edit/`; **PARTIAL** means a script covers only part of the gate; **TO BUILD** means nothing exists yet.

**Threshold labels**, carried from the modules: ES (the edit standard), USER (user-confirmed), HOUSE (a value proposed during the first test; for per-client choices now a style default), "v3c-calibrated" (measured on an early test edit; re-baseline per client), DERIVED (translated from a qualitative rule), NORM (named industry norm), VIDEO (number stated in a transcript).

**Units.** Frames are at the source rate; the numbers here assume 23.976 fps (the test footage), so 1 f = 41.7 ms. Modules that quoted "±33 ms at 30 fps" are restated here in frames.

**Instruments the gates need** (build these first; most TO BUILD rows depend on them):
1. **Element log** `Edit/elements.jsonl`, written by `cves render`. One record per frame per element: id, class, role (L1/L2/L3/caption/cta), tags (hero, problem/solution, proof, intent, word link), x, y, scale, rotation, alpha, ink bbox, font path, size, weight, fill, align, group. This merges M1's motion log and M3's text log.
2. **Face track**: `cves prep` (faces) keeps YuNet's 10 landmark values (`faces[i][4:14]`) next to the box.
3. **Stems**: voice, music, SFX and pre-limiter mix from `cves mix`, plus the SFX/event list in the mix JSON.
4. **Beat map** `beats.json` (sentence → role, connectors, tags: not built yet) and the cut list `Edit/edl.json` (built by `cves select`).
5. **Final-file probes**: ffprobe; decoded 8-bit YCbCr frames of the delivered encode (not the PNGs); whisper.cpp re-transcription of the final mix.
