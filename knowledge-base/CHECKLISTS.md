# Checklists

Binary checklists, one per stage of an edit, merged and de-duplicated from the B.3 checklist of every module, plus ES and the user's confirmed corrections. Tick an item only when it is true. Any "no" is fixed before the next stage, or logged with a reason. Square brackets give sources; `XXX-nn` ids point to `QC-GATES.md`. Items that name a per-client value (palette, grade, captions, transition kinds, cutaway share, bed level) are checked against the client's style profile (`docs/STYLE.md`); the numbers given are the defaults or, where marked, preset `profitcast-dark`.

## 1. Brief and setup
- [ ] The inspiration edits for this ad family have been reviewed frame by frame, the reference frames are listed, and the style profile has been measured from them (`cves inspire` → `Edit/style.yaml`) or chosen deliberately. [USER]
- [ ] The ad's emotion is written in one line. [2.1]
- [ ] The viewer's want and its obstacle are written down. [6.1]
- [ ] A real, specific proof (client name plus number) exists, or the client has been asked for one. [6.8, 4.6]
- [ ] Any scarcity, deadline or bonus in the offer is confirmed as true. [6.9, 4.7]
- [ ] Colour taboos for the client's community have been checked. [3.7]
- [ ] Feeling and personality words for the client are written, and colour choices trace back to them. [3.7]
- [ ] Sound moments are marked in the brief: hook, first cutaway, offer/price, CTA (≤ 2 at full intensity). [5.7, 5.1]
- [ ] Shoot notes sent: eye-level phone; locked white balance and exposure; blue, teal or denim top or a blue object behind; a neutral in frame. [7.3, 8.8, 8.5, 8.4]
- [ ] Source probed: colour tags `bt709/tv`, resolution, fps; only `v:0` / `a:0` mapped. [8.4, USER memory]
- [ ] Source resolution covers the largest planned zoom (source height ≥ 1920 × max zoom), or the softening is accepted. [7 B.3]
- [ ] The canvas is style `platform.size` (default 1080×1920, the template design space) at the source frame rate, with no conversion. [9.6, 9 B.7#8]
- [ ] The Meta Reels safe-zone overlay (14/35/6%) is loaded as a locked guide layer. [9.4]
- [ ] `cves prep` (faces) stores YuNet landmarks (eyes, nose) as well as boxes. [7 B.3]
- [ ] The renderer writes the element log (`Edit/elements.jsonl`). [1 B.5.3, 3 B.5]
- [ ] Design tokens are fixed: one font family with real weight files at least two steps apart; a ×2 size ladder; a spacing scale; a palette with one accent. [3 B.3]

## 2. Story and selects
- [ ] The raw is transcribed with word timings and read end to end. [2 B.3]
- [ ] Sentences are segmented and re-takes grouped. [6 B.2.4]
- [ ] Each kept sentence is the last complete take of its group, and it passes `cves qc` (CUT-03) (CUT-03, STORY-15). [6.2, ES]
- [ ] The chosen takes are the warmest, most energetic ones, not just the most accurate. [4.2, 4.6]
- [ ] Each kept sentence is labelled H / P / A / PR / O / C / X. [6 B.2.4]
- [ ] The ending (C) was chosen first, and the full sign-off exists in the raw (STORY-10). [6.1, USER]
- [ ] The beat order is H→P→A→PR→O→C, with at most one teaser moved up (STORY-01). [6.1]
- [ ] The connector chain is written out, with no "and" at any boundary (STORY-02). [6.3]
- [ ] No connector was "fixed" with synthesised speech. [6 B.7#5, USER]
- [ ] The topic is named in the first sentence, with no lead-in sentence or greeting before it (RET-01, RET-08). [4.1, 9.8]
- [ ] The hook's first sentence is "you/your"-framed, names a need-to-have pain, and has a contrast (RET-05). [4.1, 4.6]
- [ ] The hook is 2–3 sentences at or below a sixth-grade reading level, active voice, with one interpretation (RET-06, RET-07). [4.1]
- [ ] No vague or banned opener ("craziest", "won't believe", "watch till the end", "wait for it"). [4.1, 4.6, 9.8]
- [ ] No background or credentials line sits before the problem (STORY-13). [6.9]
- [ ] The setup→payoff table has no orphans (STORY-11). [6.7]
- [ ] Every open question is listed with the line that answers it, and every answer comes before the CTA (STORY-14). [4.2]
- [ ] Every sentence adds a new idea; no beat restates another unless it is tagged `callback` (STORY-04, STORY-05). [4.6, 6.3]
- [ ] Lists are ≤ 4 items per beat, odd counts where the script allows. [6.3, 3.2]
- [ ] Problem plus agitation stays under 45% of runtime. [6.5]
- [ ] Every stake or urgency claim is true. [4.7]
- [ ] Missing visuals or lines are flagged as pickups, not faked. [2.8]
- [ ] The user has approved the cut list.
- [ ] The words-gate reference script is the re-ordered selects script. [6 B.7#4]

## 3. Edit plan (`edit.yaml`)
- [ ] Every graphic's entry is tied to a word timestamp, or to an explicit stagger from a parent that is. [1 B.3, 2 B.3]
- [ ] Every cutaway is tagged with its line, keyword and intent (primary / secondary / context); none is wallpaper (GFX-02). [7.8]
- [ ] Elements are tagged hero, problem / solution, proof, loop open / close and zenith. [4 B.5, 6 B.5, 8.3]
- [ ] Planned A-roll shots are 2–4 s, cutaways 3–8 s (style `grammar.max_shot_s` / `max_cutaway_s`), and cutaways fill the share the style sets (`grammar.cutaway_share`, e.g. 40–55% in `profitcast-dark`) (RET-22, RET-23). [ES §1]
- [ ] No planned span without the face exceeds 8 s. [2.8, ES]
- [ ] A pace curve is sketched: the baseline average shot length, the longest hold before the peak run, the fastest list run, and a slower CTA (RET-21). [2.5, 2.6]
- [ ] There is at least one tempo change in the body (RET-20). [4.5, 4.8]
- [ ] A visual event is planned at least every 4 s (every 2.5 s in the first 6 s) (RET-16). [4 R7]
- [ ] Frame 0 has motion (push, punch-in or animated graphic) and a visual event lands by 1.0 s (RET-09, RET-10). [4.2]
- [ ] The topic appears as on-screen text by 2 s (RET-12). [4.1]
- [ ] Every uncovered A-roll cut alternates framing by ≥ 1.15× at the cut frames, or is covered (CUT-05). [ES §1]
- [ ] Every uncovered A-roll cut sits on head or hand motion. [7.5]
- [ ] The one extreme punch-in is assigned to the strongest emphasis line (FRAME-09). [7.2, ES]
- [ ] The proof boundary has the strongest visual change (STORY-16). [6.6]
- [ ] Every transition has a reason (momentum → whip, emphasis → punch, default → cut) and is a kind the style uses (`transitions.*`); no dissolves, wipes or irises, and no fades unless the style uses them. [2.3]
- [ ] Rotoscope moments: ≤ 4, each ≤ 3 s (GFX-25). [ES §1]
- [ ] The first cutaway establishes the business or place. [7.2, 7.8]
- [ ] Hero beats (price, the big number, the logo) have a calm hold planned before them. [1.1]
- [ ] No more than one primary mover is planned per moment on A-roll. [1.6]
- [ ] Each hero move is blocked as hold poses first and reads in three poses. [1.5]
- [ ] Each new element has a named origin. [1.6]
- [ ] Every animated element has a motion token (curve, frames, distance, exit). [1 B.3]
- [ ] Beat-sync is planned only for cutaway entries and graphic hits. [2.5, 9.2]

## 4. Moodboard
- [ ] Keyframes come from the production renderer at 1080×1920, not a mock-up.
- [ ] They cover frame 0, a hook frame, each cutaway type at its settle frame, one roto moment, and the CTA / end card.
- [ ] Each keyframe sits beside its nearest inspiration frame.
- [ ] The series look matches the style profile and the inspirations (stage, palette, type, cards; e.g. `profitcast-dark`: black stage, green dot grid, glow from the bottom, green and white type, cards with icon wells, check / ✕, corner brackets). [ES §2]
- [ ] Each still works on its own: one L1, clear hierarchy, balanced, aligned. [1 B.1#18, 4.3, 3.1]
- [ ] Icons are Material and client names are real wordmarks. [USER, ES §1]
- [ ] The user has approved the keyframes.

## 5. Graphics and motion
**Graphics**
- [ ] Each frame has exactly one L1 element and at most three hierarchy levels (GFX-06, GFX-07). [3.1]
- [ ] Type sizes step ×2 (×3–4 for a hero) and weights skip a step (GFX-03, GFX-04). [3.4]
- [ ] One family, or two of different classes; the wordmark is an image (GFX-05). [3.4, 3 B.7#4]
- [ ] Each card has one alignment axis, measured on ink bounds; no mixed axes in a frame (GFX-08, GFX-09). [3.4, 3.5]
- [ ] Gaps inside groups are smaller than gaps between groups, and every gap comes from the token scale (GFX-10, GFX-12). [3.5, 3.6]
- [ ] Nothing sits closer to the frame edge than to its neighbours, and nothing sits in a corner (GFX-11, PLAT-02). [3.5, 3.4]
- [ ] The accent (`colors.accent`) is only on the spoken item or CTA; accent = solution, ✕ = problem, gold (or the brand colour) = client (GFX-19, COL-13). [3.1, 8.3]
- [ ] Any off-palette colour appears once at most, on purpose. [8.8, 8.3]
- [ ] No widows, double spaces or justified text (GFX-16, GFX-17). [3.4]
- [ ] Off-centre layouts have a counterweight. [3.6]
- [ ] Spacing tempo matches the beat: tight for urgency, widest at the CTA. [3.6]
- [ ] Contrast passes on rendered frames (GFX-15). [3.1]
- [ ] Every number counts up; every list builds on its spoken words. [ES §1]

**Motion**
- [ ] No position, scale or rotation is driven by linear `prog()`, except typing, draw-on, drift and sweep (MOT-02). [1.3]
- [ ] Entries use `ease_out` or `back`; exits use `ease_in` and are no longer than entries; repositions use `ease_io` (MOT-01). [1 B.3]
- [ ] `back()` overshoot is ≤ 10% and not used on large panels (MOT-04). [1.1]
- [ ] Durations are whole frames and starts are quantised (MOT-07). [1 F2]
- [ ] Siblings share a token and are offset in time (MOT-08). [1.6, 1.2]
- [ ] Compound elements move as one parent. [1.6]
- [ ] Every element has an origin and an exit (MOT-12, MOT-17). [1.6]
- [ ] Anticipation is used only on heroes, inside the element's own word window, eased and not frozen (MOT-18). [1.5, 1 B.7#1]
- [ ] A hero graphic that moves away from where the eye is has a lead-in. [1.5]
- [ ] Moves faster than 4 px/frame have directional blur, and whips have radial blur (MOT-13, MOT-16). [1.7]
- [ ] The A-roll punch-in centre is smoothed, and scaled text is rendered once (MOT-03). [1.3]
- [ ] Holds over 1 s keep moving (MOT-15). [1.2, 4.8]
- [ ] Rasters are large enough at overshoot × supersample (MOT-14). [1.4]
- [ ] Every animation clarifies or engages; nothing is decorative. [4.3]

## 6. Framing
- [ ] The face is the brightest large region; no overlay is brighter (FRAME-08). [7.1]
- [ ] At most one non-caption overlay sits on A-roll at a time (GFX-26). [7.1]
- [ ] The eye line is on or near the top-third / golden line (FRAME-04). [7.1]
- [ ] Headroom is minimal, with no wasted band of wall (FRAME-05). [7.1]
- [ ] The face is centred horizontally, or balanced by a graphic (FRAME-06). [7.7]
- [ ] A glance or point off-lens is answered by the next shot, from that side. [7.5]
- [ ] The cutaway's first mover starts near where the eyes were (FRAME-07). [7.6]
- [ ] No rotation and no mirroring in any A-roll transform (FRAME-10). [7.4]
- [ ] The background darkening matte is 1 px erode / 3 px feather, with no aura (FRAME-03). [ES §2]
- [ ] The body touches only the frame edges (FRAME-02). [ES]
- [ ] No zoom upscales beyond the source, or the upscale is accepted (FRAME-11). [7 B.7#8]

## 7. Sound
- [ ] The voice went through `cves select` (voice chain in `engine/cves/ingest.py`) (DeepFilterNet3, EQ with a ~80–100 Hz high-pass, de-ess, compressor, expander) with no generative step (SND-30). [ES §3, 5.8]
- [ ] Voice, music and SFX are separate stems. [5.5]
- [ ] The voice is dry and centred, with no ambience under the A-roll. [5 B.2]
- [ ] Dialogue level is consistent sentence to sentence (SND-22). [5.5]
- [ ] Every speech gap is ≤ 0.25 s, and breaths are 0.08–0.15 s (CUT-02). [ES §3]
- [ ] The music suits the ad's tempo and character, and isn't a generic "safe" track. [2.6, 5.2]
- [ ] Music was re-levelled from its import level, with its own dynamics evened out. [5.5]
- [ ] If stems exist, any instrument fighting the voice is muted. [5.2]
- [ ] The bed sits at the style's `sound.bed_lu` under the voice over speech (default −10 LU; −7 LU in `profitcast-dark`), within −2.5 / +3 LU of it (SND-04). [USER]
- [ ] The duck is a frequency split: voice band −8 dB, lows and highs −3 dB, dynamic (SND-06). [ES §3, 5.6]
- [ ] The bed swells in no-speech spans of 1 s or more (SND-21). [5.5]
- [ ] Music enters on a riser into a downbeat, edits on beats, has no mood change, and exits on a tail or hit at the sign-off. [5.1, 5.4]
- [ ] There is one track, with an energy lift at the turn and no hard switch. [4 B.7#10]
- [ ] The music lift lands on the reaction to the proof number, not on the cut (SND-25). [6.6]
- [ ] There is one SFX palette (clean UI/digital, real recordings). [5.4, 5.1]
- [ ] Every SFX transient lands on its frame, and transition SFX lead into the cut (SND-07, SND-08). [5.1]
- [ ] Every cutaway and card has sound (SND-14). [5.1]
- [ ] There are ≤ 2 hero accents, layered low/mid/high; others are single subtle layers (SND-10). [5.1, 5.4]
- [ ] Repeated actions have ≥ 3 variants, with no identical consecutive repeats (SND-09). [5.1]
- [ ] Stems are gain-staged with headroom before the limiter (SND-23). [5.5]

## 8. Colour
- [ ] Balance is corrected on neutrals before `grade()`, with no global saturation fix. [8.5]
- [ ] Any white-balance multiply is in linear light. [8.5]
- [ ] The tone curve is monotonic (COL-12). [8.7]
- [ ] Saturation was re-checked after any contrast change. [8.7]
- [ ] The vignette comes after balance, centred on the face, and is felt rather than seen. [8.4, 8.7]
- [ ] No teal split-tone on the A-roll. [8.8]
- [ ] No grain, no print LUT, no `tune film`. [USER, ES §2]
- [ ] Skin hue, ΔE and exposure pass across every cut; per-segment gain is applied where needed (COL-03, COL-06, COL-07). [8.4, ES §2]
- [ ] Cutaways use only the style's palette colours (stage, card, accent, text, gold for client wordmarks) (COL-11). [8.6]
- [ ] The accent appears on every solution element and no problem element, peaking at the end card (COL-13). [8.3]
- [ ] A split contact sheet (one face crop per segment) has been checked. [8.4]

## 9. Captions and platform
- [ ] Captions are on and match style `captions:` (default: small, white, sentence case, soft shadow, no pill), phrase-level and ≤ 2 lines (target 1) (PLAT-04, PLAT-09, PLAT-10). [ES §2, 9.5, 4.8]
- [ ] Each caption line is ≤ 32 characters, ≤ 20 cps and on screen ≥ 0.83 s (PLAT-05 to PLAT-07). [9.7, NORM]
- [ ] Captions are synced within ±2 f (PLAT-08). [9 B.5]
- [ ] Captions sit where `captions.position` says (default just below the chin), clear of every take's face box, with the bottom edge inside the safe zone (≤ y 1248 by default). [9.5, 9 B.7#1]
- [ ] All text, logos, prices and the CTA sit inside style `platform.safe_zone` (default x 65–1015, y 269–1248) and `platform.margin` (default 60 px) (PLAT-01, PLAT-02). [9.4, ES]
- [ ] Frame 0 has the face or hook text, not black or a logo (RET-13). [9.8]
- [ ] The first spoken word starts within 0.25 s (RET-03). [9 B.5]
- [ ] The brand is the last word; the tail is 0.1–0.5 s or the end card ≤ 3 s; no black at head or tail (PLAT-11, PLAT-12). [6.8, 9.8]
- [ ] There is one CTA, at the end, after the payoff. [9.8, 4.2]
- [ ] Full bleed at the platform's aspect with no letterbox; other ratios are reframes of the master. [9.2]

## 10. Delivery
- [ ] Single encode from lossless frames: x264 crf 16, no `tune film`, bt709 tags with `range=tv` (DEL-05, COL-01). [ES §2]
- [ ] Style `platform.size` (default 1080×1920), SAR 1:1, the source frame rate (constant), H.264 High yuv420p, AAC 48 kHz ≥ 128 kb/s, `+faststart`, within the brief's duration (`qc.duration`) (DEL-01 to DEL-04). [9.6, NORM]
- [ ] Loudness is style `sound.master_lufs` ±0.5 (default −14 LUFS integrated) and TP ≤ `sound.true_peak` (default −1 dBTP), measured on the full file (SND-01, SND-02). [ES]
- [ ] Every BLOCKING gate passes, and every WARNING is listed in the report.
- [ ] Contact sheets have been checked at every transition: first visible, overshoot peak and settle frames (DEL-06). [ES, 1 B.3]
- [ ] Watched on a phone with sound on, as a first-time viewer. [2.1]
- [ ] Watched with sound off: the story reads from picture and captions. [2.7, 9.5]
- [ ] Watched with the Reels UI overlay: nothing important is covered. [9.4]
- [ ] Watched in a scroll mock: the first second stands out from neighbouring posts. [4.2]
- [ ] Watched as a loop: last frame to first frame is a clean cut. [9.1]
- [ ] Listened on a phone speaker and headphones, with an earshot critique run on the final mix. [5.5, ES §5]
- [ ] The click-on-every-cut review render has been heard, and build → burst → release is audible (DEL-07). [2.5]
- [ ] A fresh viewer can say the problem, the proof and the ask. [6.2]
- [ ] The QA-REVIEW sheet is complete.
- [ ] The delivery note says what was not verified.

## 11. Feedback loop
- [ ] Every user note is logged and turned into a rule (RULEBOOK or ES) and a gate or checklist line.
- [ ] Each fix targets the cause, checking upstream first. [2.1]
- [ ] Only failed gates and noted items were changed; nothing that passed was re-cut by taste. [6.6]
- [ ] CALIBRATE thresholds touched by this ad have been re-measured on the inspirations and logged.
- [ ] After launch, Meta hold rate (3-s views ÷ impressions), ThruPlay and retention drop points are recorded against beat boundaries. [4.4, 6 B.7#8, 9 B.7#9]
